What is GetMoved?

GetMoved is the operating platform for modern moving companies — combining a customer marketplace, a full SaaS ERP, and AI-powered inventory into a single connected system.

FOR CUSTOMERS

Film a walkthrough, get an AI-generated inventory, receive binding quotes from trusted movers, and pay only after your move is complete — no calls, no paperwork, no surprises.

FOR MOVING COMPANIES

Receive qualified leads, submit digital quotes, manage your crew and fleet, track every job from booking to payment — and get paid automatically when the job is confirmed.

INTELLIGENT AUTOMATION

AI analyzes your walkthrough video and automatically creates a complete inventory — items identified, quantities counted, volume and weight estimated, and the right truck size recommended. All from a single video.

BUILT FOR SCALE

Use the full platform or integrate the AI engine into your own TMS or CRM via API. Enterprise on-prem deployment available. Designed from the ground up to support multi-company, multi-market operations.

HOW IT WORKS

From first contact to final payment — the complete workflow for customers and moving companies alike.

01 — Request

Tell us about your move in a few simple steps.

  • Enter your pickup and delivery addresses
  • Choose your moving date and the services you need
  • Add your inventory — manually or by uploading a quick video
  • Submit — your request goes out to qualified movers instantly

02 — AI Inventory

Film a quick walkthrough of your home — our AI does the rest.

  • AI identifies every item in the video and builds a complete list
  • Each item is counted accurately — no duplicates, no missed pieces
  • Total volume and weight are calculated automatically
  • The right truck size is recommended based on your load
  • You can review, adjust, or add items manually at any point

03 — Compare Offers

Qualified movers review your request and send back their best offer.

  • Your request is matched to movers who cover your route and services
  • Each mover sees your full inventory and submits a binding quote
  • All quotes arrive in one place — easy to compare side by side
  • Pick the offer that works best for you and confirm

04 — Move & Pay

Your move is confirmed, tracked, and paid — without any extra coordination.

  • The mover prepares crew and vehicle based on your confirmed inventory
  • You track the job status in real time from your phone
  • Both sides confirm completion when the move is done
  • Invoice is generated automatically — no manual billing
  • Payment is held securely and released only after confirmation
