Available on Mobile

The full GetMoved experience — in your pocket. Customers book moves and track jobs. Moving companies manage quotes, crew, fleet, and payments. All from the same app.

For Customers

  • Request a quote in 4 simple steps — origin, services, inventory, send
  • Upload a walkthrough video — AI builds your inventory automatically
  • Add items manually or combine both methods for full accuracy
  • Review quotes from trusted movers with full pricing breakdown
  • Accept a quote and pay securely in one tap — card or bank
  • Track your move and booking in real time
  • Add extra services or items after booking without restarting

For Moving Companies

  • View all incoming quote requests from your mobile dashboard
  • Review full job details — route map, inventory, services, customer info
  • Set itemized pricing per service and submit binding quotes
  • Manage your vehicle roster and crew members from the app
  • Confirm jobs, track bookings, and assign resources on the go
  • Price and approve post-booking extras submitted by customers
  • Full Finance tab — invoices, payments, and job history in one place
