Find & Compare Trusted Movers in New York

Moving in New York is its own kind of challenge. Fifth-floor walk-ups, buildings that demand a Certificate of Insurance before a mover can set foot inside, and streets where a truck can't idle for more than a few minutes — in New York, the logistics matter as much as the muscle. GetMoved was built to take that stress off your plate.

Instead of calling a dozen companies and waiting around for in-home estimates, you film a short walkthrough of your home on your phone. Our AI turns that video into a detailed inventory — every box, sofa, and wardrobe counted, with volume and truck size estimated automatically. Trusted New York movers use that inventory to send you binding quotes you can compare side by side.

You choose the mover, the date, and the price. Payment is held securely and released only after your move is complete — so there are no surprise fees on moving day and no guesswork. Just a clear picture of what your New York move will cost, before anyone shows up at your door.

Common New York Moves We Handle

Apartment & walk-up moves

No elevator? Movers on GetMoved quote with your building's stairs and floor already factored in, so the price you compare is the price for your actual move — not a generic estimate.

Co-op & condo moves

Most NYC buildings require a Certificate of Insurance (COI) and a reserved elevator window before move-in. Trusted movers can provide the COI your building management needs and work within your reserved time slot.

Studio & one-bedroom moves

The most common New York move. A quick video walkthrough is all it takes to get accurate, binding quotes for a small apartment — no in-home visit required.

Parking & loading logistics

Narrow streets, no loading dock, alternate-side rules — New York movers who quote through GetMoved plan the loading logistics up front, so moving day runs on schedule.

NYC to New Jersey moves

Crossing the Hudson is one of the most common local moves in the metro. Compare movers who handle the tunnels, bridges, and both-sides logistics as a single job.
