GetMoved – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GetMoved (“GetMoved,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use our website, applications, APIs, and services.

By using the Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information

Including:

  • Name;
  • Email address;
  • Phone number;
  • Billing information;
  • Business information;
  • Address information;
  • Government or licensing information for moving companies.

B. Moving and Inventory Information

Including:

  • Origin and destination addresses;
  • Inventory lists;
  • Uploaded photos and videos;
  • Moving schedules;
  • Property information.

C. Technical Information

Including:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type;
  • Device information;
  • Operating system;
  • Cookies and tracking data;
  • Usage analytics.

D. AI and Platform Data

Including:

  • AI-generated inventory estimates;
  • User interactions with AI tools;
  • Operational and logistics data.

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information:

  • Directly from users;
  • Through website interactions;
  • Through mobile applications;
  • Through integrations and APIs;
  • Through cookies and analytics technologies;
  • From service providers and partners.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Operate and improve the Platform;
  • Match customers with movers;
  • Process transactions;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Improve AI systems and automation;
  • Analyze Platform performance;
  • Detect fraud and security threats;
  • Comply with legal obligations;
  • Send operational communications;
  • Provide marketing communications where permitted.

4. AI Processing

Uploaded content such as photos, videos, and inventory information may be processed using artificial intelligence technologies.

This processing may include:

  • Object recognition;
  • Inventory detection;
  • Volume estimation;
  • Automation and optimization;
  • Operational recommendations.

Users acknowledge that AI systems may involve automated processing and estimation.

We may share information with:

  • Analytics providers;
  • Technology vendors;

We do not sell personal information in exchange for monetary compensation.

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Personalize user experience;
  • Support advertising and marketing.

Users may adjust browser settings to manage cookies.

We retain information for as long as necessary to:

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information.
